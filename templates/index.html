Model Card

Intended use & limitations

Intended Use

Advisory estimation of red and white wine quality from physicochemical laboratory measurements.

Key Limitation

R² = 0.532. Wine quality is partly subjective, so laboratory measurements cannot explain every aspect of sensory quality.

Decision Boundary

Predictions should support human judgment, not replace professional sensory evaluation.

Not Intended For

Regulatory approval, product certification, safety decisions, or automated acceptance and rejection of wine batches.
